Privacy

Tonewell collects nothing about you.

No accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking: not in the apps, and not on this site.

What stays on your device

Your settings, the sounds you save and the session history the app keeps are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What touches the internet

  • The phone apps never connect to the internet. The links to sources in Learn open in your browser, only when you tap one.
  • The desktop app asks tonewell.space once a day whether there’s a new version, and downloads updates from GitHub. You can turn this off in Settings. These requests carry nothing about you, beyond the IP address that every web request reveals and that servers may briefly log.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. “Hear a taste” makes its sound in your browser.

Buying the phone app

Google Play or the App Store handles the purchase. Tonewell never sees your payment details; the store gives the developer only its usual sales records.

Permissions

On Android, Tonewell runs a media playback service so the sound carries on with the screen locked, and shows a notification with the session’s controls. It doesn’t ask for your microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

On a Mac, “Soften during calls” checks whether any app is using the microphone. Tonewell never records or listens.

Children

Tonewell isn’t directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.
