# What to listen for

The automated checks (`npm run test:audio`) measure levels, beat
frequencies, glides and fades, but they can't tell whether something sounds
right. These are the things worth checking by ear, with headphones, at a
comfortable volume.

## Deep Theta (the first Tonewell sound)

- **The first ten seconds:** silence opening into the drone, with no click
  or jump at the start.
- **The drone:** a warm, slightly reedy chord (root, fifth, octave) with a
  slow brightening and darkening about every half-minute as the filter
  sweeps. There should be a gentle beating between the two detuned root
  voices, never a harsh buzz.
- **The wander:** every 40–60 seconds the whole chord glides to a nearby
  note (A, then F, G, E). It should feel like a slow lean, not a step.
- **Bells:** sparse, soft, from different sides, each with a faint echo.
  None should poke out louder than the others or sound clipped at the start.
- **Breath:** a quiet airy rush that rises and falls, sitting behind the
  drone.
- **The beat:** with headphones, a soft wobble in the low hum. For the first
  six minutes it slows from about ten wobbles a second to six. Take the
  headphones half off and it should disappear (that's how you know it's
  binaural).

## Wind down

Press Esc (or Wind down). Over about six seconds:

- the bells and the beat fade away;
- the drone darkens to a low, warm hum and gets a little quieter;
- nothing should drop out suddenly or click.

Press Resume: the sound should bloom back over about four seconds. Press Esc
twice (a second apart): it should fade to silence over six seconds.

## Timers

- Wind a 10-minute session. At the end you should hear one soft bowl-like
  bell, then the sound winds down to the hum, rests for two minutes, and
  fades out over twenty seconds.
- In Focus with a 25 + 5 cycle, a soft bell marks the start and end of each
  break; during the break the sound settles to the hum.

## Focus presets

- **Brown Noise Desk:** deep, even, no movement at all. Nothing should
  pulse or swell.
- **Rain Room:** fine patter over a soft hiss with a low drone underneath.
  Drops should feel random, never rhythmic.
- **Shoreline:** waves arriving every 8–15 seconds and drawing back.
- **Warm Drone:** almost still. If you notice it changing, the sweep is too
  fast.

## Voices (Journey)

A wordless choir: soft voices on "oo", "ah" and a hummed "mm", fading in
and out on notes of the chord. Listen for anything that sounds like a word
or a syllable: there shouldn't be any. Voices should sit back in the room,
not in front of it.

## Things that should never happen

- A sudden loud sound, at any time, including when starting, switching
  presets or changing modes.
- Sound coming out of the speakers when headphones are unplugged: Tonewell
  should go quiet at once and wait.
- Volume jumping when you drag the volume slider fast to the right: it
  should rise over a second or two.
- Crackles or dropouts with the window hidden or the screen locked.
